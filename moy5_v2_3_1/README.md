# Co-reading explainer — moy-5, received version v2.3.1

> **What changed from the v2.2 bundle** (2026-11-09): **v2.3** draws, inside the cone overlay (states 0 and 7), the **cutting plane** — the plane through the line FVD (line j) perpendicular to the board — with the parabola drawn in it, the focus F, the vertex V and the directrix through D (drawing from the frozen coordinates, not data). **v2.3.1** **fixes** that plane and its parabola at the frozen P_x: moving P_x now moves only the lifted point P_y along the same parabola (past the plane's edge as a thin dashed continuation); in v2.3 the plane was re-sized each time (about 29 times in area). The data and the numbers are v2.2's; the anchors grow from 434 to **447** (13 for the plane) and the calibration from 18 to **19** kinds (plus 3 / 3 planted faults in the plane caught). Six stills were re-taken; `data/` holds the same 95 files as the v2.2 bundle.

**Station 3 of 3** in the gallery: *moy-5 — how to make a model draw the auxiliary line*. (Station 1: [moy-2 — from algebraic analysis to elementary geometry](../moy2line_v1_3/README.md); station 2: [moy-1 — elementary geometry](../moy1_v3_1/README.md).)

An audited, interactive figure of **where the apex appears** when frontier models answer one parabola problem (moy-5: the point P_y moves on a parabola; the relation at the apex is a **mean proportional** — Menaechmus's *symptoma*, Newton's Lemma XIV): in most arms the answer field states only the floor (the frozen invariant EJ = KP_y) and the apex lives in the reason field; it reaches the answer field when the question's point set is changed, when roles + the proportion form are given, or at effort max. Each dot is one real answer (one jsonl row). The figure then shows **what the house tested after it** — 20 frozen cards (olm60–82, thk1 / thk2) reading the frontier's road inside OLMo-3 32B — graded by a fixed rule, and the next questions the readers proposed. **31 states in 12 chapters**, Japanese / English, dark / light, also an anywidget for Jupyter / marimo; 69 stills at 1920 × 1080; a two-voice script; a ledger of what the figure can and cannot say. **v2.2** adds, on the board (states 0 and 7), an overlay that shows the 2-D board as the plane through the axis of a cone (apex C, generatrix CJ) with the Dandelin sphere centred at A, and **v2.3 / v2.3.1** add to it the cutting plane through the line FVD, perpendicular to the board, fixed at the frozen P_x — drawing computed from the frozen coordinates, not data; it can be hidden. Every number on the page is anchored to the frozen data (`check_claims.py`, **447 anchors**); the frozen files the figure reads are **copied into this bundle** (`data/`), so the numbers can be recounted here without the house repository.

## What is not said

The figure's ledger is states 28–30 and `CLAIMS*.md` (the × rows and the L3 rows). In short: an answer field on the floor is not evidence that the model lacks the apex (say neither absence nor possession); the OLMo readings describe OLMo-3 32B base reading the frontier's text, not the frontier models; the "three regimes" (Pappus · Menaechmus · Descartes) are a reading, not a verdict of the data; the later cards' grades are SCOPE 10-31's tiers or the RESULT's frozen predicates; the cone overlay and its cutting plane are drawing and say nothing about a model.

## Reading glosses (for the verbatim Japanese cards, the page text and the maker's notes)

- *sensei* (the Japanese word for "teacher", written in kanji) — the principal investigator (PI); it is how the assistant that wrote the cards addresses its principal. It is left as written in the cards and the card copies under `data/`, **and in the page text** (`i18n.js` / `figure.js` / `figure.css`, so in `dist/`: e.g. "the house fires bundle ① (*sensei* 11-07)", written in kanji), because the pages are kept byte-identical to the received version (the same choice as station 1). - **札** card · **腕** arm · **床** floor · **関門** gate · **計器** instrument · **判定** verdict · **記述** description — see `cards/INDEX_en.md`.
- **zebrashark** — the lab's build host (an M2 Ultra) named in the cards' and notes' run lines.
