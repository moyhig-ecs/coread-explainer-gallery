## 0. The acceptance form (why this station was not judged like station 1)

Station 1 (moy-2) was judged on "can the figure be read correctly". For stations 2 and 3 the PI set a different purpose (2026-11-06): co-reading is a prototype for people reading what an AI produced, and **"can you see what comes next" is the most important acceptance condition**; two makers are there to **find the variation**, not to pick a winner. The form v2 has three parts: **N** = is the next step visible (the condition), **D** = the difference between the two makers, **G** = an audit gate (the AI's job; it does not decide acceptance).
